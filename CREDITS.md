# Media credits

The demo schedule uses a small library of free-license clips and photos. The same file is reused across the three-month calendar, with a new caption each time it comes around. Optimized copies live in `seed-media/`. Each video is H.264, 720 by 1280, with no audio. Motion clips are 4 seconds. Photo clips are a 3 second still.

Pexels and Pixabay video files returned HTTP 403 from this environment, so the motion clips are Mixkit videos under the Mixkit Stock Video Free License. Photos are from Pexels and Unsplash.
